1. Release the flexible cable (1a) from the side retainers (1b).

2. Release the flexible cable from the retainers in the centre area.

3. Remove the flexible cable (1a) releasing the rod (3b) from the retainer (3c).

If necessary, separate the rod (3b) from the flexible cable (1a).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing
