E03 CLUTCH VIBRATING OR JERKING

Applies to: engines: 1.2 16v; all production periods

The clutch pedal vibrates and 'jerks'. When released, the vehicle does not start smoothly, but jerks or vibrates

First check:

  • engine idle speed

Applies to: engines: 1.2 16v; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON CLUTCH PEDAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the clutch pedal travel

Continue to Step 2

Pedal travel not correct

Restore the operation of the clutch pedal

2

ENGINE SUPPORT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the centering of the front crossmember and the engine supports and connecting rod

Continue to Step 3

Engine supports fastening faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to torqueOp. 1008A10

Faulty link attachment

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to torque

3

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • that there is no clutch plate wear or 'vitrification'
  • that there is no damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring

Continue to Step 4

Fault in clutch plate

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + rIf there is oil, replace the gearbox input oil sealOp. 2110C204

Faulty clutch pressure plate

Replace clutch pressure plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

4

CHECK SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the clutch plate hub, gearbox main shaft and the condition of the assembly

Continue to Step 5

Clutch plate hub faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

Gearbox main shaft faulty

Replace the gearbox main shaft Op. 2110B20 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers and internal controls if necessary

5

FLYWHEEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the flywheel (in particular, the state of the surface, making sure that there is no overheating, uneven wear, incisions or material removed), operation of the flexible couplings

Continue to Step 6

Flywheel damaged

Replace the flywheel Op. 1024B22 engine flywheel - r r with clutch or gearbox removed

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
