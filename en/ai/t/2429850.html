Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The steering wheel is made from mangnesium to make it lighter and stronger. Height adjustable steering wheel. The steering column is fixed to the mounting bracket which is strengthened considerably by a tranversely mounted bar which secures the bracket to prevent vibrations and resonance. The steering column has a telescopic sliding structure for improved safety.

The steering box is constant ratio type (51 mm/fixed revolution) while the servo mechanism is electric and located on the steering column.
