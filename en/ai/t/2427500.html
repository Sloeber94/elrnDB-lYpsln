H30 ELECTRIC STEERING NOT WORKING / CITY

When the CITY button is pressed, the function is not activated and greater power assistance for the electric steering is not obtained

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

FUNCTIONAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

When pressing the CITY button, check that:

  • The LED in the actual button comes on.
  • The corresponding symbol in the NQS lights up.

Continue to Step 2

Signal in button and symbol off constantly.

Continue to Step 5

The LED in the button is constantly off whilst the symbol lights up

Continue to Step 3

The LED in the button lights up, but the symbol does not.

Continue to Step 8

2

CHECK ON CITY FUNCTION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a road test to check that a difference in power assistance takes place when the CITY button on the control panel is pressed

End of diagnosis

LED and symbol are working whilst the servo assistance does not alter

Continue as instructed See table H25 NO ELECTRIC STEERING POWER ASSISTANCE WITH FAILURE WARNING LIGHT NOT ON

Problems met
