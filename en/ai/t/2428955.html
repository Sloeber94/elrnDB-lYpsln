Removing

Place the fuel vapour active charcoal filter with integral pipes back in its housing.

Secure the fuel vapour recirculation pipe to the body using the fastenings.

Tighten the nuts securing the fuel vapour active charcoal filter.

Connect the safety lead to the body using the pin.
