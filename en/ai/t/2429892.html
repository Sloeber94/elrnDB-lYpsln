Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Low setup

Functional description

The motors for the door mirrors P60 and P61 are operated by the controls H44 located on the driver's door.

Driver door control unit H44 contains a joystick and door mirror control switch.

These controls H44 are supplied at pin 6 of connector A by the line protected by fuse F49 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2.

The shared power supply for the mirror motors comes from pin 4 of connector A whilst the commands for moving the left and right mirrors P60 and P61 come from pins 1-4 and 3-2 of connector B; the mirror motors are operated in this way at pin 5 (shared) and at pins 3 and 4 (horizontal and vertical movement, respectively).
