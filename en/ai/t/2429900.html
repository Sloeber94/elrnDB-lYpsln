Functional description

The dipped headlamps activation signal is sent to pin 2 of connector I of the Body Computer M1 from the steering column switch unit H5 See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS .

The Body Computer M1 controls the supply to the dipped beam headlamps, sending an earth signal to the unit under the dashboard B2 from pin 8 which energizes the relay T1; this supplies the dipped beam headlamps and gives enablement to the headlamp adjustment motors F10 (left) and F11 (right).

The headlamp adjustment control, located in the unit H90, sends a control signal - when the relevant buttons are pressed - to the instrument panel E50 - pin 11 of connector B: the internal logic of E50 then sends the actual control signal to the motors, from pin 5 of connector A, while pin 6 of connector A receives the enablement signal (dipped beam lights on).

These control signals flow through underfacia control unit pin 1 of connector A of B2 to the motors in headlamps F10 and F11.
