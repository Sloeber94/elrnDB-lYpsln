1048A14 air cleaner filtering element - replace.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Release the side fastenings and remove the air filter cover (1a) with cold air intake (1b).

2. Remove the air filter filter element.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 16v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts fixing the air filter assembly.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the air filter cover (2b).

3. Remove the air filter filter element.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 16v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts fixing the air filter assembly.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the air filter cover (2b).

3. Remove the air filter filter element.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the air filter cover (1b).

2. Remove the gasket.

3. Remove the air filter filter element.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

.

Before fitting a new filter element, remove any impurities in the filter casing (by suction) that could fall into the clean air section of the circuit. It is also advisable, when refitting, to take care to ensure that the filter element is correctly positioned making sure that the seal is in its housing and is not cut or damaged. This is to prevent irregular engine operation.

Fit a new filter element in its seating.

Refit the air filter cover with cold air intake and secure with the side fastenings.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 16v; all production periods

Removing

.

This is to prevent irregular engine operation.

Place a new filter element for the air filter back in the housing.

Close the air filter cover and fix it using the bolts.

Tighten the air filter bolts.
