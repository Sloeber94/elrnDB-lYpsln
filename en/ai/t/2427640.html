The diagnostic equipment signals the following error: Misfire in cylinder X

Carry out the procedure for the circuit for the cylinder in which the error was detected

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the ignition circuit is correctly connected and working properly, paying particular attention to:

  • wiring;
  • connectors;
  • high tension leads continuity See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Continue to Step 1

Faults in the electrical circuit

Restore correct connections. See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENTIf necessary, replace the faulty components.

Continue to Step 11

1

IGNITION COIL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the Examiner 180636900, visually check the operation of the coil connected to the cylinder concerned.

In particular, check that the spark is regular

Continue to Step 2

The coil spark is not regular

Continue to Step 10

2

SPARK PLUG CONDITION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the electrode gap for the spark plug concerned is within the permissible limits.

Also check that the electrodes are not worn/broken

Continue to Step 3

Spark plug faulty

Replace the spark plug. Op. 5510D10 spark plugs - r+r

Continue to Step 11

3

CHECK ON FUEL PRESSURE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Test the fuel pressure Op. 0510C10 fuel system - check delivery pressureby turning the ignition ON and waiting for a few seconds.

Start the engine normally and after a few seconds turn the ignition off.

Check that the pressure remains at normal values and that it has not fallen by more than 1.5 bar 15 minutes after the engine has been turned off.

Remove the injector flute complete with all the injectors . Op. 1056B60 single fuel manifold pipe, with injectors - r + r

Place a container under the injector flute.

Continue to Step 6

Pressure is too low: constrictions on the supply pipe

Renew or, if necessary, replace the pipe.

Continue to Step 11

Excessive pressure drops after switch-off

Continue to Step 4

Low pressure: faults in the fuel pump unit

Replace the fuel pump assembly Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 11

4

CHECK INJECTOR SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Turn the ignition on and after a few seconds turn it off again.

Check that there are no fuel leaks from the injector concerned.

Refit the injector flute Op. 1056B60 single fuel manifold pipe, with injectors - r + r

Work very carefully and in safe conditions

Continue to Step 5

The injector leaks

Replace the injector Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt

Continue to Step 11

5

REPLACING FUEL PUMP

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Replace the fuel pump assembly Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 11

6

INJECTOR ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out active diagnosis on the injector concerned, checking that during activation there is spray from the injector.

Refit the injector flute Op. 1056B60 single fuel manifold pipe, with injectors - r + r

Continue to Step 7

Injector stuck shut

Replace the injector Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt

Continue to Step 11

7

CYLINDER COMPRESSION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the cylinder compression test Op. 0510A10 cylinder compression - check for loose spark plugs or injectors

Continue to Step 11

Compression insufficient for the cylinder involved

Continue to Step 8

8

CHECKING VALVE CLEARANCE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the valve tappet clearance Op. 1036H14 valve tappets - check clearance

Continue to Step 9

Clearance not correct

Restore correct clearance Op. 1036H18 VALVE TAPPETS - Adjust. following clearance check

Continue to Step 11

9

CHECK ON CAUSE OF INSUFFICIENT COMPRESSION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out R/R on head to check the valve seal

Check the condition of the cylinders/piston rings

End of diagnosis

Insufficient valve seal

Overhaul the cylinder head Op. 1016E10 single cylinder head, removed - overhaul

Continue to Step 11

Cylinders/piston rings worn

Renew the seal.

Continue to Step 11

10

COIL REPLACEMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Exchange the coil concerned with one from another cylinder Op. 5510C10and check, using the Examiner 1806369000 that the spark is regular

Continue to Step 11

There is no regular spark

Renew the old coil and replace the engine management control unit Op. 1056B82 injection/ignition system e.c.u. (one) - r + r

Continue to Step 11

11

ERROR CANCELLING AND CHECKING

Operation
