Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the switch is pressed the seat backrest adjustment is not working.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check for the following at the CAN:

  • inactive control units;
  • control unit errors;
  • control units with errors in CAN line.

Continue to Step 2

Faults present

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keep the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected and check for errors:

  • at the seat backrest adjustment control switch;
  • at the seat backrest adjustment operating relay.

Continue to Step 4

Seat backrest adjustment control switch error.

Continue to Step 8

Seat backrest adjustment operating relay error.

Continue to Step 3

3

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check continuity/insulation of the electrical connection between the seat backrest adjustment operating relay and the driver's position node/passenger position node See E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS.

Continue to Step 6

Electrical conection broken or not insulated.

Restore electrical connection continuity/insulation See E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS.

4

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the seat backrest adjustment motor is working properly using active diagnosis.

Continue to Step 8

Seat backrest adjustment motor not working.

Continue to Step 5

5

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check continuity/insulation of the electrical connection between the seat backrest adjustment operating relay and the driver's position node/passenger position node See E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS.

Continue to Step 7

Electrical conection broken or not insulated.

Restore electrical connection continuity/insulation See E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS.

  • if the front passenger seat is involved: passegner position node
