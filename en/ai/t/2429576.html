7040A26 steering column trim - r+r

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the bottom trim.

3. Undo the bolts.

4. Release the steering column position adjustment lever (4a) and lower the steering column, then remove the upper cover (4b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the trims are not damaged.

Position the upper cover, lock the steering column adjustment lever and tighten the bolts.

Position the lower cover and fix it using the bolts.
