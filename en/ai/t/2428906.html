Refitting

Place the car on a ramp.

With the ignition key in the OFF position, disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Raise the car.

1. Undo the filler fitting retaining bolts.

2. Open collars (2a) and disconnect fuel filler pipe (2b) and pipe (2c) from the tank.

3. Undo the fixing bolts (3a) and remove fuel filler pipe (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods
