Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: High setup

Description

The door mirrors are adjustable electrically in both directions by means of a joystick; located in the door controls, driver's side: a slide switch selects the right or left mirror.

The button, which can be pressed in four directions, controls two electric motors located in each of the two mirrors: one motor rotates the mirror on a horizontal axis, while the other on a vertical axis.

The circuit is protected by a fuse in the underfacia control unit and controlled by the door control unit in accordance with a control strategy transmitted via the CAN network from the Body Computer.
