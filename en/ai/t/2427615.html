With the car in motion, a smell is noted coming from the rear of the car, particularly after refuelling

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First of all, check the fuel tank for leaks or damage

Continue to Step 1

Leaks from the fuel tank

Repair the fuel tank, or replace if necessary (Op. 1040A22)

1

CHECK TANK FILLER FITTING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tank filler fitting, in particular:

  • The seal of the filler housing on the wing;
  • The condition of the filler;
  • The cap seal

Continue to Step 2

Filler faulty

Renew or replace the filler ( Op. 1040A30 fuel filler pipe - r + r)

Cap faulty

Renew or replace the filler ( Op. 1040A40 plug for fuel filler pipe - r + r)

2

CHECK PUMP COVER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seal of the pump/fuel gauge cover gasket

Continue to Step 3

Gasket worn, cover damaged

Replace the gasker or cover ( Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r)

3

VAPOUR RECOVERY SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the vapour recovery system seal

Continue to Step 4

Leaks from vapour recovery system

Replace the damaged components

4

CHECK BOOT FRAME SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that boot is sealed correctly.

Continue to Step 5

Boot seal faulty

Restore correct boot seal

5

UNDERBODY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the underbody plugs in the boot area are present

Continue to Step 6

Underbody plugs missing

Refit the missing plugs

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
