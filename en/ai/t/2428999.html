Refit the throttle body with integral MDS and secure with a bolt.

Place the mounting bracket, complete with injection-ignition system control unit, back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Connect the electrical connection to the throttle body integral with M.D.S.

Proceed with refitting 1048A32 sleeve from air cleaner to intake manifold - r + r .

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.
