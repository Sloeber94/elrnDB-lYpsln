1060B Low fuel pressure supply circuit check

Path

Kind
test / check procedure

Interactive page

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD; all production periods

Connect the pressure transducer for the Examiner diagnostic equipment between the diesel filter [1a] and the pressure pump [1b] (connect to the filter outlet).

Turn the ignition on.

Check, within 10 seconds, that the pressure gauge reading is between 2 and 3 bar.

The pump in the tank stops after 10 seconds.
