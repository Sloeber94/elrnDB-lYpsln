Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

The sump has the task of housing the engine lubrication oil; it is made from pressed steel and includes the port and plug for draining the oil. The seal with the crankcase is ensured by a bead of silicon sealant.

The timing side and flywheel side covers guarantee the crankshaft seal and are fixed to the crankcase by bolts.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

The sump has the task of housing the engine lubrication oil; it is made entirely from aluminium and includes the threaded port and plug for draining the oil.

The seal with the crankcase is ensured by a bead of silicon sealant. The timing side and flywheel side covers guarantee the crankshaft seal and are fixed to the crankcase by bolts.
