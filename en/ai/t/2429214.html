Removing

Refitting

Proceed with removal 5580C18 air bag module - r+r .

Follow all the recommended safety rules 5580C

Position the front wheels perfectly straight and mark the position of the steering wheel in relation to the upper casing.

1. Remove the protective ring.

2. Undo the nut (2a) and remove steering wheel (2b).

The steering wheel fixing nut must be replaced each time it is dismantled.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the steering wheel in its housing check that the marks made previously on the steering wheel and the upper half casing are aligned.

Tighten a new steering wheel fixing nut to the recommended torque.
