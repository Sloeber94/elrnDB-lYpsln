Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The rear seats are fitted with squabs built to ensure maximum occupant safety in the case of various types of impact. They also prevent items in the luggage compartment from being propelled forward.

Backrest and seat may be different types:

  • single backrest and seat with centre head restraint and sliding functon,
  • split backrest and seat with sliding function and adjustable angle backrest.

The squab recline catches are located near the joint.

The rear cushions come with a pressed steel base with antislip profile that forms an effective antisubmarining structure in conjunction with the beam welded to the rear floor pan. On the sliding version, the movement mechanisms are fastened to the base of the bodyshell. On versions with split rear seats, the backrest can be folded separately, 40/60%.

The seats are fitted with two head restraints for the side seats. These can be adjusted to two positions. Certain versions come with a third head restraint for the central seat with the same specifications. Versions with a third head restraint also come with a central inertia reel seat belt.

On certain versions, the rear seats may be sliding with adjustable squab recline.

Specifications

One-piece seat and squab with central head restraint and slide function, with split seat and squab with slide function and squab with adjustable recline. The squab recline catches are located near the joint.

The rear cushions come with a pressed steel base with antislip profile that forms an effective antisubmarining structure in conjunction with the beam welded to the rear floor pan. On the sliding version, the slide mechanisms are secured to the body floorpan and controlled by Bowden cables with handles fixed in the central part of the seat. Central grab handle for full seat slide manoeuvre, right grab handle for reclining or adjusting the squab on the same side, left grab handle for reclining or adjusting the squb on the same side. There is a release handle, near the wheel arch, for folding the backrest from the luggage compartment.
