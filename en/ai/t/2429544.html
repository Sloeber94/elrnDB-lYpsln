Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the nuts fixing the lever.

2. Support the lever slightly.

3. Disconnect the cable (3a) from the retainers (3b).

4. Keep the lever.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the lever is undamaged.

Connect the cable to the retainers.

Position the lever and fasten it using the nuts.
