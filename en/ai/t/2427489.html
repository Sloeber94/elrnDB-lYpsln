All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the handbrake cables are fixed correctly and are not stiff

Continue to Step 3

Cables attached incorrectly

Restore the attachments

Cables hardened

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the efficiency of the play take-up device

Continue to Step 4

Device jammed

Change the device

CHECK ON RATCHET GEAR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the handbrake lever ratchet gear is working properly

Button jammed

Ratchet gear jammed

Free or replace the operating lever ( Op. 3380A10 handbrake lever - r+r)

Contact surfaces inadequate
