Refitting

Place the vehicle on a lift.

Drain the coolant ( 0010T84 coolant for air conditioning system dehydrate and recharge ).

7210E10 R.R. FRONT BUMPER

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and disconnect the connector (1b ) for the pipe from the condenser to the expansion valve.

2. Undo the bolt (2a) and disconnect the connector (2b) for the pipe from the condenser to the compressor.

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

Refitting

Removing

Check that the condenser is intact and is not damaged and does not have deposits of dirt on the finned surface.

If removed, refit the drier filter (refer to op. (5040A44))

Place the condenser in position and attach it to the lower retainers.

Tighten the upper condenser fixing bolts.

1. Connect the connector (1a) for the pipe from the condenser to the compressor and secure it by tightening the bolt (1b) to the recommended torque.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors.

Only use green coloured seals, resistant to R134A coolant fluid.

Lubricate the thread of the connectors using anti-freeze.
