Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The car is fitted with a horn system which consists of two horns with different tones: a high tone and a low tone horn. Both horns are activated simultaneously.

The horns are operated by pressing the sides of the steering wheel cover (around the Air Bag).

The horn circuit is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a specific relay located in the engine compartment control unit.

For safety reasons, the horns can be operated at any time, even when the key is turned to OFF (or removed).
