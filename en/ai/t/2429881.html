Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The connection for the vehicle for connecting the trailer D73 receives a direct supply, at pin 1, from the line protected by fuse F36 of junction unit B2 .

An inhibition signal, on the other hand, is sent via pin 2 to pin 14 of the parking sensors control unit M84 See E3090 PARKING SENSOR , which disables the operation of this system.

If the connection D73 is not supplied, the wiring for the fitting kit should contain a terminal connected directly to the supply protected by fuse F36 - pin 13 of connector D of B2 and a terminal connected to the parking sensors control unit M84, pin 14.
