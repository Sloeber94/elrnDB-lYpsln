PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the fault is accompanied by a noise from the gearbox with the vehicle stationary and in neutral

Check the gearbox/differential fluid level (Op. 2110A04)

Continue to Step 1

Level not correct

Restore the correct level (Op. 2110A04). If the defect persists,

Continue to Step 1

1

LEAKS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Thoroughly wash the gearbox, cover it with talc and road test the vehicle.

Visually check that there are no leaks

In particular, check the condition of the gaskets:

  • gearbox input shaft gasket
  • rear cover gasket
  • differential oil seal

Continue to Step 2

Leaks from the seals

Replace the gaskets (Op. 2110C22)

Left differential flange leaks

Replace the flange ( Op. 2110D52 l/h flange on differential - r + r with drive shaft removed)

Right differential oil seal leaks

Replace the oil seal ( Op. 2110D63 rh differential shaft oil seal - r r)

2

GEARBOX CASING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually check the condition of the gearbox casing, using talc

Continue to Step 3

Casing damaged, cracked or distorted

Replace the gearbox casing, the rear cover, or the entire gearbox ( Op. 2110B32 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers, inner controls, gears, shafts and bearings)
