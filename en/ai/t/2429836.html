Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

The emission control system includes devices designed to keep down the level of harmful emissions in the atmosphere.

The main vehicle emissions are:

  • exhaust emissions
  • vapour/gas emission from the crankcase
  • fuel vapour emissions from the fuel circuit
