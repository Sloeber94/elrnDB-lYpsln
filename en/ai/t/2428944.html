Refitting

Removing

Using a file, remove the bevel on the cut end of the central exhaust pipe.

1. Refit in its seating the new rear exhaust silencer.

2. Join the rear exhaust silencer to the central exhaust pipe using the metal band supplied by Parts with the rear exhaust silencer.

3. Tighten the nut on the metal band.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods
