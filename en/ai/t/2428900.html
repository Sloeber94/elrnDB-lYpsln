Refitting

Proceed with removal 1048A10 air cleaner filtering element - replace .

Proceed with removal 1016A20 single cam cover - r + r and replace gasket .

Act on the drive pulley bolt using a suitable spanner so that the valves are in the closed position.

With the engine cold, check, using a feeler gauge, that with the valves in a closed position the clearance is within the recommended values.
