Front and rear side lights are fitted with two bulbs per light. If even one bulb is not working, replace and check using the internal light circuit

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the wiring between the body computer node and affected light. See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 2

Wiring short circuited or with circuit open

Renew the electrical connections See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

2

CHECKING LIGHT ELECTRICAL SYSTEM

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check internal light connections See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 3

Connections short-circuited

Restore to operation and change light if necessary

3

CHECKING BUILB CONDITION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of both bulbs

Continue to Step 4

Bulbs faulty

Replace bulbs

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Light does not come on

Replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32
