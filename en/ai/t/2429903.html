Functional description

The radio P20 is supplied directly (4 of connector A) from the battery via the line of fuse F39 of the unit under the dashboard B2.

Pins 1 and 3 of connector A receive the CAN line with which the radio P20 is connected to the Body Computer M1 and the other nodes of the system. Pin 8 of connector A is earthed.

The aerial P25 is connected to the radio also via the specific coaxial cable and is supplied by pin 5 of connector A of P20.

Pin 6 of connector A of P20 receives the supply for the lighting (with the side lights on) of the actual radio display, pin 40 of connector E of M1.

Radio P20 is not connected to the radio controls on the steering wheel: the connection is via the clock spring D47 , which is a node of the CAN network E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES - Functional description .

Connector B of P20 sends out cables carrying signals to the speakers:

  • pins 5 and 6 to left front loudspeakers P35 and P45 ,
  • pins 3 and 4 to right front loudspeakers P35 and P46 ,
  • pins 7 and 8 to the left rear loudspeaker P30 ,
  • pins 1 and 2 to the right rear loudspeaker P31 ,

Connector E of P20 has the cable connected to the CD player P75 coming out of it.
