Refitting

Removing

1. Lay both parts of the pillar on the car correctly and secure using self-locking grippers.

Check the alignment and ensure the surrounding gaps are even.

2. Use a spot welder to weld the areas shown in the figure.

3. Use a MIG welder to fill the holes made previously.

4. Make a few tacks only in these areas: the part will be welded fully when the rear wing is fitted.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
