1040A32 fuel filler pipe union sleeve - r + r

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 4450B08 right rear wheel r r .

Proceed with removal 7055B45 right hand rear supplementary wheel arch - r+r .

1. Undo the bolts fixing the fuel filler pipe to the bodyshell.

2. Loosen the band (2a) and disconnect the fuel filler pipe (2b) from the tank.

3. Loosen the band (3a) and disconnect the fuel return pipe (3b).

4. Remove the sleeve joining the fuel filler pipe to the tank.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 16v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a lift.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the fuel filler mounting bracket.

2. Remove the retaining band (2a) and disconnect the fuel filler pipe to the tank (2b).

3. Remove the retaining band (3a) and disconnect the drain pipe (3b).

4. Remove the sleeve joining the fuel filler pipe to the tank.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods
