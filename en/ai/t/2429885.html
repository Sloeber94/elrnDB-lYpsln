Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The system for the horns is supplied through the horn relay T3 of the engine compartment control unit B1 from the direct line from the battery through the fuse F10 located in B1 .

The relay coil is energized by an earth signal when one of the steering switches is closed; these switches are connected via the clock spring to the stalk unit D47 .

The relay supplies the horns P05.
