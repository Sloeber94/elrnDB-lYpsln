Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD; all production periods

If replacing the compressor, proceed as follows.

1. Drain the fluid contained in the compressor throught the openings connected to the pipes. If necessary, rotate the compressor several times and rotate the pulley, manually, to remove all the fluid.

The fluid is extremely hygroscopic: avoid leaving the containers open. Avoid leaving the compressor or any other component disconnected from the system for longer than is strictly necessary.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.2 16v, 1.4 16v; all production periods
