Refitting

Removing

Place the lower radiator crossmember complete with engine cooling radiator and radiator cooling fan back in its housing using the hydraulic jack.

Tighten the bolts fixing the lower radiator crossmember.

Tighten the bolts fixing the air conditioning compressor to the radiator cooling fan.

Tighten the bolt fixing the radiator cooling fan to the bodyshell.

Connect the radiator upper coolant inlet hose to the radiator and tighten the band.

Connect the electrical connection to the radiator cooling fan.

Connect the electrical connections to the resistor for the engine cooling fan.

Connect the lower coolant outlet hose from the radiator, radiator side, and tighten the band.

Proceed with refitting 7210E10 R.R. FRONT BUMPER .

Proceed with refitting 1048A10 air cleaner filtering element - replace .

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.

Refill the engine cooling system with coolant 0010T20 coolant change .

Remove the car from the ramps.

Refitting

Removing

Install the engine cooling radiator on the lower radiator beam

Refit radiator cooling fan, securing the lower pins to the lower radiator beam and press-fitting the tabs to the radiator.

Tighten the side bolts fixing the radiator cooling fan.

Place the lower radiator crossmember complete with engine cooling radiator and radiator cooling fan back in its housing using the hydraulic jack.

Tighten the bolts securing the radiator bottom crossmember to the bodywork.

Remove the hydraulic jack from underneath the lower radiator crossmember.

Tighten the upper bolt fixing the engine cooling radiator to the bodyshell.

Tighten the bolts securing the air conditioner condenser to the engine cooling radiator.

Refit the electrical wiring in its seating and attach it.

Connect the electrical connections to the resistor for the engine cooling fan.

Connect the electrical connection to the engine fan motor.

Connect the radiator upper coolant inlet hose to the radiator and tighten the band.

Connec the system supply pipe, radiator side.

Connect the lower coolant outlet hose to the radiator and tighten the band.

Proceed with refitting 7210E10 R.R. FRONT BUMPER .

Refill the engine cooling system with coolant 0010T20 coolant change .

Proceed with refitting .

Remove the car from the ramps.
