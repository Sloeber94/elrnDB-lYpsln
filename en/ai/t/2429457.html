Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Before removing and refitting the Air Bag module, the following preliminary operations must be carried out.

Place the ignition key in the OFF position and extract it;

- Disconnect the battery leads and insulate.

- Wait for 10 minutes before continuing.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY FULES (see 5580C - Precautions).
