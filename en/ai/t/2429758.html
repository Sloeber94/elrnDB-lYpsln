Refitting

Removing

Remove the spark plugs for cylinders 1 and 2.

1. Position the tools (1a) in the housings for the spark plug for cylinders 1 and 2, tightening the components (1b) to a torque of 0.5 daNm.

2. Rotate the crankshaft in its normal direction of rotation, using small movements, until the surface (2a) on the moving part of the tool is aligned with the plane (2b) on the fixed part in the spark plug housing. When both tools positioned in the spark plug housing are in this condition the pistons will be aligned, i.e. halfway through their stroke and the piston for cylinder 1 will be descending, i.e. in the inlet stroke.

The pistons were already approximately aligned previously.

Rotate the crankshaft gradually to prevent the pins for the tools being expelled by the piston compression.

The camshafts were timed previously through the fitting of the tools (3b).
