Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Brake lights do not work The third brake light works normally

The RH and LH brake lights are controlled directly from the Body Computer Node on the basis of information from the Brake Light switch.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Check that there are no faults in the Body Computer

Continue to Step 2

Brake lights faulty.

Check the individual lights.

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECK ON INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and complete bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 3

Bulbs damaged/unsuitable

Replace bulbs

Bulbs damaged/unsuitable

Renew or replace the rear light cluster

3

BRAKE LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • stop lights fuse
  • connection between dashboard control unit, Body Computer and brake pedal switch;
  • connections between Body Computer and bulbs
  • bulb earth : See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 4

Defective component

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections

Faulty earth

Restore the earth contact See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

4

CHECK ON BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the brake pedal switch is fitted correctly and working See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 5

Switch fastening / adjustment incorrect

Adjust the switch

Brake pedal switch faulty

Replace the switch

5

CHECKING FACIA CONTROL UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity of the facia control unit internal circuit See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 6

Facia control unit circuit broken

Change the facia junction unit

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
