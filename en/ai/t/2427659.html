(*)Or every 3 years if the vehicle is used in one of the following particularly harsh conditions: prolonged use in hot/cold climates;

- prolonged use in cold or hot climates;

- town driving with a lot of idling;

- use on particularly dusty roads or roads covered in sand and/or salt

(**) Every 12 months in the case of lower mileage.

It is advisable to change the engine oil more often than indicated in the Planned Maintenance Programme

Planned if the vehicle is mainly used in one of the following conditions:

- towing a trailer

- dusty roads

- short (less than 7-8 km) and frequent journeys at sub-zero temperatures

- engine often running at idle speed or vehicle driven for long distances at low speed (or prolonged inactivity).
