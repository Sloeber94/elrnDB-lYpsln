Refitting

7040A18 glove compartment on facia - arrange

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the cover (2a) releasing the retainers (2b) and (2c).

3. Extract the filter element.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the filter element is not damaged or clogged.

1. Fi the filter element (1a).

The referenc arrow (1b) (AIR FLOW) should be positioned as illustrated in the diagram (in the direction of the air flow).

2. Attach the retainers (2a) for the cover, then tighten the bolts (2b).
