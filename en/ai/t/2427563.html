Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the ignition is turned on, the 'electric steering faulty' warning light does not come on under any circumstances.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check, in the Instrument Panel Node ambient, that there are no errors and the following parameters are present.

Continue to Step 7

Impossible to connect with the NQS control unit

Continue to Step 2

There are errors

Continue to Step 5

2

ELECTRIC POWER SUPPLY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the Instrument Panel Node electrical power supply is correct See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 3

Positive and/or earth electrical power supply faulty.

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

3

CHECK ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the electrical connections for the Instrument Panel Node, in particular making sure that each individual pin is correctly connected and inserted in its housing.

Continue to Step 4

NQS electrical connections not working

Restore damaged electrical connections.

4

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the Examiner, check the possibility of connecting with the Instrument Panel Node

Continue to Step 1

Impossible to connect with the control unit

Replace the Instrument Panel Node Op. 5560B11

5

ERRORS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the Examiner in Instrument Panel Node ambient, check that the specific error is not present

Continue to Step 7

Error detected inside the NQS control unit

Continue to Step 6

6

CHECK WITH ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the Examiner in the Instrument Panel Node ambient, check, in Active Diagnosis, that the function in question is correctly activated

Continue to Step 7

The warning light does not come on

Replace the Instrument Panel Node control unit Op. 5560B11

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
