Removing

Refitting

Proceed with removal 4450D70 hub cap of one rear wheel, l/h or r/h - r+r .

1. Undo the pins (1a) and remove the rear brake drum (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the nut (1a) and remove the rear wheel hub (1b) complete with spacers (1c).

The rear wheel hub bearing should not be removed from the hub. If it proves necessary on account of noise or excessive clearance, replace the rear wheel hub complete with bearing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the rear wheel hub (1a) complete with spacers (1b).

2. Tighten the nut fixing the rear wheel hub to the recommended torque.
