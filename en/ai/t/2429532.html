7015A24 tailgate hinges - repair or replace with tailgate removed

Removing

Refitting

7065C30 interior l/h or r/h grab handle (one) - r+r (rear)

1. Work on both sides of the vehicle, release the covers (1a) and undo the bolts for the rear pillar trims (1c).

2. Lower the roof lining (2a) gently and undo the hinge bolts (2b).

3. Remove the hinges.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the hinges are undamaged.

Fit the hinges and screw in the bolts without tightening them.

With the tailgate fitted 7015A14 tailgate - adjust centring and profile - includes hinge and compartment seal adaptation

Place the roof lining and the rear pillar trims back in position, then fasten them using the bolts and attach the covers.
