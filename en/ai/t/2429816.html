Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Passenger's Air Bag and Side Bag

If the error does not reappear after it is cancelled the test is finished

Disconnect the bag connector on the line involved and activate the relevant line closure resistor

Cancel the error and check it has disappeared using a testing device such as Examiner.

Reconnect the bag connector.

Check the continuity of the wiring for the control unit connector on the light connection panel connector: there should be no DC interruptions.

If there are no faults, the control unit is faulty.
