Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The turn signals on one side, LH or RH, do not work;

The turn signals are controlled and diagnosed (light operation check) directly by the body computer node.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON BULBS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the bulbs and that the rating is correct.

Continue to Step 2

Bulbs damaged

Replace bulbs

2

CHECK ON STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the steering column switch unit, in particular the direction indicators and lane change function

Continue to Step 3

Control not working

Replace the steering column switch unit

3

CHECK ON DIRECTION INDICATORS/HAZARD LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • connections between stalk unit and Body Computer
  • connections between Body Computer and bulbs
  • bulb earths: See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS

Continue to Step 4

Defective component

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections

Lamps earth defective

Renew the bulb earth: See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS

4

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the tester input and check:

  • the operation of the direciton indicator bulbs through active diagnosis

Continue to Step 4

Bulbs do not come on

Replace the Body Computer

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
