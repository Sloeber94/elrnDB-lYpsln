1. Use a grinding wheel to remove the corrosion-proofing from around the inner and outer edges of the replacement side member lining.

Offer up the replacement part and check that it matches. Trim it, if necessary.

2. Drill out the holes indicated.

Apply electric weld paint finish to the edges to be welded.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Position the side member lining correctly on the vehicle and secure it using self-locking clamps.

3. Use a MIG welder to fill the holes made previously.
