Removing

Refitting

Lower the window.

1. Disconnect the cable (1a) from the retainers (1b).

2. Use a drift and remove the pin (2a) removing it upwards Keep the spring (2b) and the handle (2c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the handle is undamaged.

Fit the handle.

Refit the spring (1a) in place with the ends (1b) and (1c) in the housings shown in the diagram to produce a slight pre-loading at the end of the fitting process.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Fit the pin in position.

Connect the cable to the retainers.

Test the operation of the handle.
