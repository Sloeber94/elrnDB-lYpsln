Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fuel consumption indiator in the display (one of the TRIP COMPUTER module displays) does not work with te ignition ON.

The display shows two types of fuel consumption, instantaneous and average. The fuel consumption figures are processed by the instrument panel node on the basis of the information received from the body computer node (distance) and from the engine management node (litres consumed), made available to the instrument panel node via the B-CAN and the C-CAN, respectively.

If there is no speed information (from the Brake Node) or instantaneous fuel consumption (from the Engine Management Node) the consumption figure will be replaced by '----'

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
