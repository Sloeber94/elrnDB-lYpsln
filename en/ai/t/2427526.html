Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON HEADLAMP ALIGNMENT CORRECTOR ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • connection between centre control panel and NQS;
  • connection between NQS and Dashboard Control Unit;
  • connection between dashboard control unit and headlamp alignment motors;
  • headlamp alignment motor and headlamp alignment control earth: See E3080 HEADLAMP AGLINMENT CORRECTOR

In parameter mode

  • the plausibility of all the parameters.

In active diagnosis mode:

  • operation of ABS warning light

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the connections: See E3080 HEADLAMP AGLINMENT CORRECTOR

Connections not correct

Restore the earth contacts : See E3080 HEADLAMP AGLINMENT CORRECTOR

Continue to Step 3
