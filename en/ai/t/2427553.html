Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.2 16v, 1.4 16v; all production periods

The MIL failure warning light flashes when the engine has been started

The warning light can flash in two different ways: with the vehicle started up and stationary or with the vehicle driving.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.2 16v, 1.4 16v; all production periods
