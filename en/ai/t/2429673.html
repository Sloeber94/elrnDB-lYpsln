Removing

Refitting

1. Using a plastic blade, remove the badge (1a) unsticking the double sided tape (1b) from the perimeter and releasing the centering pins (1c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the crest is undamaged.

1. Clean the badge housing, removing the residual adhesive and degrease with heptane.

2. Remove the protective film (2a) for the double sided tape and place the badge in position and apply pressure.
