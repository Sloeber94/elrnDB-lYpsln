The warning light remains on with the driver's seat belt fastened.

The seat belt unfastened warning light is turned on by a direct earth control between the switch and the Instrument Panel Node.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • connections between switch and Instrument Panel Node;
  • switch earth

Continue to Step 2

Restore the correct connections

Switch earth not working properly

Repair the earth contact

All OK
