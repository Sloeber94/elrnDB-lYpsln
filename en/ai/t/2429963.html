WARNINGS

Use care when working on trim components and use the specified tools correctly in order to avoid damage. After fitting a trim section, carefully mould the trim onto the padding.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRECAUTIONS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRECAUTIONS

Before working on the electrical system (connectors, electrical components, wiring, etc.) turn the ignition off (STOP) and DISCONNECT THE BATTERY.

If the seats are fitted with side Air Bags, observe the specific safety regulations (see 5580C).
