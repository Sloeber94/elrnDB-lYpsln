Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

FOR LEFT BRAKE DISC

Proceed with removal 4450B02 left front wheel r r .

FOR RIGHT BRAKE DISC

Proceed with removal 4450B04 right front wheel r r .

Proceed with removal 3310A48 front brake disc - r r .

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the front brake disc shield (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the front brake disc shield back in its housing and fix it using the bolts.

Proceed with refitting 3310A48 front brake disc - r r .

FOR RIGHT BRAKE DISC

Proceed with refitting 4450B04 right front wheel r r .

FOR LEFT BRAKE DISC

Proceed with refitting 4450B02 left front wheel r r .

Remove the car from the ramps.
