Check the engine oil level with the car level and engine off. Wait a few minutes (5 minutes) after the engine is turned off.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

1. Remove the engine oil dipstick, clean and then fully reinsert. Remove and check the level is between the MIN and MAX limits.

2. If the oil level is close to or even below the MIN level, add the recommended oil through the oil filler opening up to the MAX mark.

When the engine oil level is over the MAX level following a normal check, adjust the level.

After topping up the engine oil, allow the engine to run for a few seconds, then turn off and leave for a few minutes before checking the level again.
