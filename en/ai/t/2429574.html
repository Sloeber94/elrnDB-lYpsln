Removing

Refitting

1. Open the dashboard compartment flap.

2. Release the rods (2a) from the housings (2b).

3. Undo the bolts fixing the compartment.

5. Recover the compartment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the compartment is not damaged.

Position the compartment and tighten the bolts.

Attach the rods in the housings.

Test the operation of the flap.
