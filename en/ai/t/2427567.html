Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fault warning light is on and the passenger air bag is not excluded from the control selector

The passenger airbag disablement warning light is controlled directly via a dedicated link to the Instrument Panel Node from the airbag control unit

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the tester, check the airbag control unit for:

  • system/component faults
  • disablement control plausibility

Continue to Step 4

System/component fault

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Command not plausible

Continue to Step 2

2

ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity and the insulation of the wires between the selector control on the passenger side facia and the airbag control unit. See E7030 AIRBAG

Continue to Step 3

Wiring short circuited or circuit open

Replace wiring See E7030 AIRBAG

3

CHECKING CONTROL SELECTOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the correct operation of the selector on the passenger side facia

Continue to Step 4

Selector not working

Change passenger air bag disablement selector Op. 5580C26 LOCK BARREL KEY/SWITCH for deactivating passenger 'AIR BAG - R.R.

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Change airbag control unit Op. 5580C14 air bag ecu - r+r
