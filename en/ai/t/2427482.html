During acceleration, a consistent increase in the engine speed is noticed without a corresponding increase in the speed of the vehicle

Applies to: engines: 1.2 16v; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the engine running, press the clutch pedal and engage 4th speed Accelerate and gradually release the clutch pedal checking that the engine revs do not increase during this manoeuvre whilst the vehicle remains stationary.

Continue to Step 1

The vehicle does not move and the engine speed increases

Continue to Step 1

1

CLUTCH ASSEMBLY - FLYWHEEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • clutch plate wear
  • clutch plate fouling
  • that the clutch plate is not distorted
  • condition of diaphragm spring
  • condition of flywheel (in particular the condition of the surface, uneven wear, signs of overheating)

Continue to Step 2

Plate worn

Replace the assembly Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

Plate fouled with oil or grease

Replace the clutch plate, clean the assembly, eliminate oil leaks Op. 1810A24 clutch - r + r with gearbox removed

Disc distorted

Replace the assembly Op. 1810A24 clutch - r + r with gearbox removed

Spring broken

Replace the assembly Op. 1810A24 clutch - r + r with gearbox removed

Flywheel with signs of grooves, wear or overheating

Replace the flywheel Op. 1024B22 engine flywheel - r r with clutch or gearbox removed

2
