5580CF Passenger bag disabled check

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Passenger's Air Bag

Check that the control unit shows a resistance of 100 Ohm when the device is switched to ON.

Check that the control unit shows a resistance of 400 Ohm when the device is switched to OFF.

Check the continuity of the wiring between the passenger side bypass device and the control unit: there should be no interruptions

If there are no faults in the device and the wiring, the control unit is faulty.
