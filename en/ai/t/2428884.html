2. Rotate the crankshaft in its normal direction of rotation with small movements until the moving parts of the tools are aligned.

When both tools positioned in the spark plug housings are in this condition, the pistons will be in line, i.e. halfway through their stoke and the piston for cylinder 1 will be descending, i.e. in the inlet stroke.

Rotate the crankshaft gradually to prevent the pins for the tools being expelled by the piston compression.
