Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

FOR THE LEFT LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM

Proceed with removal 4450B02 left front wheel r r .

FOR THE RIGHT LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM

Proceed with removal 4450B04 right front wheel r r .

1. Undo the bolt securing the lower track control arm to the front hub steering knuckle.

2. Disconnect the lower track control arm (2a) from the steering knuckle using a suitable tool (2b).

3. Undo the bolts fixing the anti-roll bar to the lower track control arm.

4. Undo the bolts fixing the anti-roll bar to the front suspension crossmember.

5. Undo the bolt (5a) and the bolt (5b) fixing the lower track control arm to the front suspension crossmember.

6. Remove the lower track control arm.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

.

To ensure the correct operation of the front suspension and prevent premature damage of the track control arm rubber bushes, the torque tightening of the bolts fixing the lower track control arm to the crossmember should be carried out keeping the track control arm in the position determined by the static load of the vehicle in condition Standard A: complete vehicle mass, including spare wheel, tools, accessories and supplies (full fuel tank).

1. Replace the lower track control arm in its housing.

2. Tighten the bolt (2a) and the bolt (2b) fixing the lower track control arm to the front suspension crossmember.
