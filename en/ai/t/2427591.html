Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the relevant switches are pressed, the front driver and passenger side windows do not work.

The power supply to the door nodes, that control the window electric motors, is protected by special fuses that are housed in the underfacia junction unit and governed by a relay, also located in the underfacia junction unit
