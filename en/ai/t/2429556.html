Removing

Refitting

Fully open the sun-roof.

1. Using a special tool, release the spoiler (1a) from the fastenings 91b) and remove it.

2. Undo the fixing bolt (2a) and remove the spoiler lifting spring (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the spoiler lifting spring in place and tighten the fixing bolt.

Position the spoiler and secure it in the housing in the frame.
