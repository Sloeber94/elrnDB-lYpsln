CHECKING CYLINDER HEAD

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the cylinder head;

That that the following are absent:

  • Wear of the valve stem with excessive clearance between the valve stems and valve guides and between the valve stems and oil seals
  • Oily dross on the valves;
  • Defects in the cylinder block/crankcase plane;
  • Defects in the cylinder head gasket

Continue to Step 2

Excessive clearance between the valve stem and valve guide and between the valve stem and oil seal

Replace the damaged/worn components

Presence of oily dross on the valves

Overhaul the cylinder head Op. 1016E10 single cylinder head, removed - overhaul

Cylinder block/crankcase plane defective

Reface the cylinder block/crankcase

Cylinder head gasket faulty

Check that the planarity between the head/crankcase matching planes is correct and replace the head gasket

ENGINE OIL PENETRATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the piston seals, in particular:

  • that they are not broken or damaged
  • that the actual rings are correctly fitted
  • that there is no seizing or sticking in the grooves

Continue to Step 3

Piston seals damaged or seized

Replace pistons, pins and seals

Piston seals not correctly fitted

Fit the seals correctly

3

CYLINDER LINER/BORE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the cylinder liners/bores. In particular:

  • That there are no grooves and/or vitrification
  • Dimensions of the liners/bores, in particular that there is no ovality/taper/wear
  • Correct piston - liner/bore fitting clearance

Continue to Step 4

Liners/bores with grooves and/or vitrified

Grind the cylinder liners/bores

Dimensions outside the tolerance: presence of ovality/taper/wear

Grind the cylinder liners/bores

Fitting clearance incorrect

Replace the pistons, pins and seals, following the fitting clearances
