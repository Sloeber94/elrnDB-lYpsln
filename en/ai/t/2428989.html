Removing

Refitting

Proceed with removal 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with removal 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

1. Loosen the collar and disconnect the engine oil vapour intake pipe from the tappet cover.

2. Disconnect the engine oil vapour inlet pipe from the intake manifold.

3. Loosen the band, disconnect the hose from the air filter to the intake manifold, intake manifold side and remove it.

4. Loosen band and disconnect the upper coolant inlet hose to the radiator thermostat side.

Recover the coolant which comes out in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 16v; all production periods
