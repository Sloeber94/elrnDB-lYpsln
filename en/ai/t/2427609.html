Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle moving, there is noise coming from one of the front doors.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Firstly check the moving window glass is properly closed.

Check that the noise is actually coming from one of the front doors by carrying out the preliminary check for locating noises.

Continue to Step 1

The wind noise comes from the bonnet

Continue according to the instructions in See table T05 BONNET WIND NOISE

The wind noise comes from the windcreen

Continue according to the instructions in See table T06 WINDSCREEN WIND NOISE

The noise is coming from the lower area of the passenger compartment

Continue according to the instructions in See table T10 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT WIND NOISE, LOWER AREA / (UNDERBODY)

The wind noise comes from the sun roof

Continue according to the instructions in See table T20 WIND NOISE FROM THE SUN ROOF

The noise is coming from the rear area

Continue according to the instructions in See table T25 WIND NOISE FROM THE REAR AREA

1

FRONT DOOR WIND NOISE LOCATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the detailed check for locating wind noise from the front doors ( See Test 7005A Location of front door wind noise) and identify the area where the noise is coming from.

Continue to Step 2

The noise persists with the entire door taped

Check for sealant between the wing and the pillar under the wheel arch

The wind noise is coming from the area between the pillar and the windscreen

Check the condition of the door compartment seal in that area and ensure it is bonded properly to the body, if necessary, replace it ( Op. 7005A34 front side door compartment seal r r)

The wind noise is coming from the area between the door and the windscreen

Check the condition of the door compartment seal in that area and ensure it is bonded properly to the body, if necessary, replace it ( Op. 7005A34 front side door compartment seal r r)

The wind noise is coming from the area between the door and the windscreen

Adjust the door hinges ( Op. 7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment)

The wind noise is coming from the area between the door and the wing

Adjust the door hinges ( Op. 7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment)

6. The wind noise is coming from the area of the glass scraper trim

V6. Check the glass scraper is undamaged and correctly fitted Op. 7005D26 front side descending window glass scraper (outer) r r

6. The wind noise is coming from the area of the glass scraper trim

Check the condition of the seal between mirror and door and make sure it is correctly fitted. Adjust or replace the actual mirror

2

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
