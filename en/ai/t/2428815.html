Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.2 16v, 1.4 16v; all production periods

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Check that the tyre treads are not unevenly worn.

Some types of tyres are fitted with wear indicators; they have to be replaced as soon as these indicators become visible.

Do not cross-interchange the tyres.

Check that the tyre pressures are correct, including the spare tyre.

The tyre pressures should be checked on cold tyres.

Check that all the lights are working: headlamps, direction indicators, hazard warning lights, boot light, courtesy light, glove compartment light and instrument panel warning lights.

Check that the windscreen wash-wipe system is working correctly and if necessary adjust the jets.

Check that the windscreen and rear window wiper blades are correctly positioned and not worn.

Check the condition and war of the front brake pads.

Check condition and wear of rear drum brake linings .

Visually check:

  • bodywork exterior;
  • underbody protection;
  • flexible and rigid sections of the pipes (exhaust - fuel system - brakes);
  • rubber components (boots, hoses, bushes, etc.).

Visually check the condition of the accessory drive belt.
