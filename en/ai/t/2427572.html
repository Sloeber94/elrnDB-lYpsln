Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With key on, the MIL warning light does not come on;

The MIL warning light is turned on via a direct earth control supplied by the engine control node. When everything is working normall, the MIL warning light should come on with the ignition on and go out once the engine has started;

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
