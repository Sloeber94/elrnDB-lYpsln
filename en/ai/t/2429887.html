Functional description

The electronic device inside the steering column switch unit H5 and the controls on the right lever are supplied at pin 11 of connector A by the line 'controlled by the ignition' (INT/A) of relay T12 protected by fuse F43, both housed in the underdashboard control unit B2.

The electronic section of the steering column switch unit H5 which manages the rearscreen wiper function is supplied at pin 12 of connector C from the same line 'controlled by the ignition' (INT/A) for the relay T12 protected by the fuse F52, housed in the underdashboard junction unit B2.

Pin 7 of connector A and pin 9 of connector C of H5 are earthed.

When the rearscreen wiper function is switched on, the steering column switch unit H5 sends the control signal to the rearscreen wiper motor N16 from pin 1 of connector B; pin 2, on the other hand, receives the earth signal coming from the motor end of travel cam which stops operation. The motor N16 is then earthed.

The windscreen/rearscreen washer switch, when operated, sends two signals from pins 10 and 12 of connector A which operate the windscreen/rearscreen washer pump N22 directly.

Pin 3 of connector A receives the automatic rearscreen wiper activation signal coming from the Body Computer M1 , pin 1 of connector A E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS .
