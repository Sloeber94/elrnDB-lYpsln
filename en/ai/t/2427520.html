Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the fog lights switch is operated with the side lights on, one of the fog lights does not work.

The fog lights are controlled by the Body Computer Node via a relay in the Engine Bay Control Unit

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of individual bulbs and lamps

Continue to Step 2

Bulbs damaged

Replace bulbs

Right front fog light faulty

Repair or replace the right front fog light

Left front fog light faulty

Repair or replace the left front fog light

2

CHECK FOG LAMP ELECTRIC CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • fog light fuse;
  • Connections between body computer and engine bay control unit
  • connections between engine bay control unit and bulbs;
  • bulb earth : See E2030 FOG LIGHTS

Continue to Step 3

Defective component

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Defective component

Restore the connections

Defective component

Restore the earth to the bulbs : See E2030 FOG LIGHTS

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
