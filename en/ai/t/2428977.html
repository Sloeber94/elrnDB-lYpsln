Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 1048A10 air cleaner filtering element - replace .

Proceed with removal 1072C44 exhaust manifold heat shield on bulkhead - r+r .

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the coolant outlet pipe from the climate control heater, water pump rigid inlet pipe side.

2. Loosen the band and disconnect the lower radiator coolant outlet hose from the radiator, water pump rigid inlet pipe end.

Recover the coolant which comes out in a suitable container.

3. Undo the bolt (3a) and remove the coolant inlet rigid pipe to the water pump (3b).

Removing
