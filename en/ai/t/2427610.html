Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle moving, there is noise coming from the front of the passenger compartment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check that the bonnet is properly closed

Check that the noise is actually coming from the bonnet lid by carrying out the test for locating the noise.

Continue to Step 1

The wind noise comes from the front dooor

Continue according to the instructions in See table T01 WIND NOISE FROM THE FRONT DOORS

The wind noise comes from the windcreen

Continue according to the instructions in See table T06 WINDSCREEN WIND NOISE

The noise is coming from the lower area of the passenger compartment

Continue according to the instructions in See table T10 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT WIND NOISE, LOWER AREA / (UNDERBODY)

The wind noise comes from the sun roof

Continue according to the instructions in See table T20 WIND NOISE FROM THE SUN ROOF

The noise is coming from the rear area

Continue according to the instructions in See table T25 WIND NOISE FROM THE REAR AREA

1

CHECK LOCATION OF NOISE FROM BONNET LID

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the detailed test for locating noise from the bonnet lid ( See Test 7025A Location of bonnet wind noise) and identify the area where the noise is coming from.

Continue to Step 2

The noise is coming from the side of the bonnet

Check the foam blocks at the point where the bonnet and wings fit together at the rear.

Check the condition of the wind seal and make sure it is properly fitted ( Op. 7025A62 bonnet lid anti-wind noise seal - r+r)

The noise is coming from the front of the bonnet

Check the condition of the front seal and make sure it is properly fitted ( Op. 7025A62 bonnet lid anti-wind noise seal - r+r)

2

FLAME DAMPER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the flame damper partition. In particular, ascertain:

  • The plugs are fitted;
  • The plugs are correctly fitted;
  • the rubber cable covers are fitted correctly
  • the sealing is efficient
  • correct fitting

Continue to Step 3

Flame damper partition faulty

Renew the flame damper partition

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
