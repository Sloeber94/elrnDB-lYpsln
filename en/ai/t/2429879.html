Functional description

The fog lights activated signal reaches pin 20 of connector H of M1 from the control button on switch panel H90.

The side lights activated signal is sent to pin 28 of connector G of the Body Computer M1 from the steering column switch unit H5E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS .

The 'ignition ON' signal (INT) for the switch H1 reaches the underdashboard control unit B2 at pin 11 of connector H and from there moves on to the Body Computer M1 (pin 9 of connector F).

The Body Computer M1 controls the supply for the fog lights by sending an earth signal to the engine compartment junction unit B1 from pin 8 of connector A of M1 which energizes the relay T14: this supplies the fog lights F15 (left) and F16 (right). The circuit is protected by fuse F30 of control unit B2 .

The Body Computer M1 is connected, via the CAN line, to the instrument panel E50 to manage the fog lights on warning light.
