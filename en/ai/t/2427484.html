When the clutch pedal is pressed, the resistance is excessive,

Since this symptom is 'subjective', make sure that there really is an effective hardening compared with the previous condition which was considered 'normal'

Applies to: engines: 1.2 16v; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the clutch pedal travel and operation

Continue to Step 2

Pedal/pedal assembly faulty

Restore the operation of the clutch pedal

2

CLUTCH PUMP AND PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the pump and the impulse damper and make sure that there are no obstructions in the clutch pipes

Continue to Step 3

Clutch actuator faulty or oil leaks

Replace the components involvedOp. 1820B48

3

CLUTCH OPERATING ACTUATOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the clutch actuator, inside the bell housing

Continue to Step 4

Clutch actuator faulty or oil leaks

Replace the components involvedOp. 1820B48

4

CHECK SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check for grease on the clutch plate hub bushes and the gearbox main shaft, and the condition of the assembly

Continue to Step 5

Clutch plate hub faulty

Restore the correct lubrication of the bushes and, if necessary, replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

Gearbox main shaft hub faulty

Replace the gearbox main shaft Op. 2110B20 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers and internal controls if necessary

5

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • clutch plate wear
  • that the clutch plate is not distorted
  • that there is no damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring

Continue to Step 6

Clutch plate or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
