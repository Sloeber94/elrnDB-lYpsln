Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the piston - connecting rod assembly (1a), complete with half-bearing, using the tool (1b).

Make sure that the arrow, stamped on the piston crown, is facing the timing side and that the number, stamped on the big end (matching cylinder), corresponds to the one of the connecting rod cap.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the piston-connecting rod assemblies (1a) complete with bearing halves (1b) using a suitable tool (1c).

Make sure that the arrow, stamped on the piston crown, is facing the timing side and that the number, stamped on the big end, corresponds to the one of the connecting rod cap.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

1. Place the connecting rod cap, complete with half-bearing, back in its housing.

2. Tighten the bolts fixing the connecting rod caps to the recommended torque using the angular tightening tool.
