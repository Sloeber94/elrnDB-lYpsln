Results of the tests

1

STEERING COLUMN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the hardness of the steering column rotation after having disconnected it from the steering box

Continue to Step 2

The electric steering column turns stiffly

Continue as instructed See table H29 STEERING HEAVY WITH POOR RETURN

2

CHECK ON RACK-PINION PLAY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the vehicle raised at the front, check the rack-pinion play with the steering column detached and the wheels straight

Continue to Step 3

No play

OverhaulOp. 4120A20or if necessary replace the steering

Excessuve steering box pinion clearance

Overhaul Op. 4120A20or replace the steering

3

CHECK ON CONDITION OF GAITERS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the front wheels and the track rod ends from the hub and check the condition of the boots

Continue to Step 4

Dust excluders broken

Lubricate the steering and replace the gaiters Op. 4120A30 l/h or r/h steering box boot (one) - replace at bench

4

CHECK ON RACK MOVEMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check how easily the rack moves

Continue to Step 5

Steering stiff

Replace the steering Op. 4120A10 steering box - r+r - check wheel tow-in if necessary separately

5

CHECK ON BUSH-RACK RADIAL PLAY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the radial play on bushes-rack

Check the fixing of the steering box

Continue to Step 6

Excessive play

Replace the steering Op. 4120A10 steering box - r+r - check wheel tow-in if necessary separately

Fixing slack

Tighten the pins to torque

6

CHECK ON TIE ROD END BALLJOINT PLAY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the play on the tie rod end balljoints

Continue to Step 7

Balljoints seized or with play

Replace the tie rods Op. 4110B10 steering links - r+r on vehicle - check tow-in/tow-out separately

7

CHECK ON TIGHT SPOTS/STICKING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

By turning the hubs, check that there are no tight spots/sticking

Continue to Step 8

Shock absorber bearing seized

Replace the bearing

Play on arm balljoint

Replace joint

8

CHECK ON STEERING WHEEL RETURN

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Road test the vehicle and check that the steering wheel returns correctly

Continue to Step 9

Poor steering wheel return

Adjust the caster angle

9

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
