Removing

Place the clutch pedal switch in its housing rotating it in a clockwise/anti-clockwise direction until the retainer tooth can be heard clicking into the seat on the braket.

Connect the electrical connection to the clutch pedal switch.

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.
