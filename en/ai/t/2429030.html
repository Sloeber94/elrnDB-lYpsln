Removing

Refitting

Proceed with removal 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with removal 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

1. Undo the nut fixing the rigid air supply pipe between the heat exchanger and the intake manifold.

2. Loosen the bands and remove the air supply hose between the rigid pipe and the intake manifold.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the air supply hose between the rigid pipe and the intake manifold back in its housing.

Tighten the nut fixing the rigid air supply pipe between the heat exchanger and the intake manifold.

Proceed with refitting 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

Proceed with refitting 5530B10 battery - r+r .
