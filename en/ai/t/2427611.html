With the car in motion, noises can be heard from the upper front area.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Firstly, check the front aerial is correctly fitted (if present).

Check whether the noise comes from the windscreen by carrying out a preliminary check to locate the noise.

Continue to Step 1

The wind noise comes from the front dooor

Continue according to the instructions in See table T01 WIND NOISE FROM THE FRONT DOORS

The wind noise comes from the bonnet

Continue according to the instructions in See table T05 BONNET WIND NOISE

The noise is coming from the lower area of the passenger compartment

Continue according to the instructions in See table T10 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT WIND NOISE, LOWER AREA / (UNDERBODY)

The wind noise comes from the sun roof

Continue according to the instructions in See table T20 WIND NOISE FROM THE SUN ROOF

The noise is coming from the rear area

Continue according to the instructions in See table T25 WIND NOISE FROM THE REAR AREA

1

CHECK LOCATION OF WINDOW NOISE AT FRONT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a check to locate window noise from the windscreen ( See Test 7035A Check there is no penetration through the fixed windows (windscreen and rearscreen))

Continue to Step 2

Seal defective

Check the condition of the outer weather strip over the glass (there should be no wrinkles or kinks)

Replace the outer weather strip if necessary by removing the glass (Op. 7035A14)

2

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
