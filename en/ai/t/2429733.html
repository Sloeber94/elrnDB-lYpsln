Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Lift the bonnet lid.

1. Protect door eges (1a), door sill (1b) and front pillar base (1c) by applying adhesive tape.

2. Undo the nut.

3. Undo the bolts.

4. Recover the washers.

5. Remove the wing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check the wing is undamaged.

Position the wing and screw in the bolts and nut without tightening.

Check the wing is aligned with the door and the bonnet lid.

7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment

Tighten the bolts and nut.
