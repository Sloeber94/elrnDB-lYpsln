Applies to: all engines; all production periods

After rain or washing, water enters the car in the region of one of the front doors

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING DOOR DRIP CHANNEL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Checking the door drip channel In particular, ascertain:

  • absence of dirt, leaves, etc.
  • corner area is properly fitted (the seal should not cover the air intake hole)

Continue to Step 2

Dirt in the drip channel

Clean the drip channel

Drip channel incorrectly fitted

Refit the drip channel properly (Op. 7055A14)

2

DOOR HOUSING SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the door seal. In particular, ascertain:

  • absence of wrinkles in the seal
  • condition of seal

Continue to Step 3

Faulty fitting of seal

Fit the sealing strip correctly ( Op. 7005A34 front side door compartment seal r r)

3

DOOR ALIGNMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fitting of the door. In particular, ascertain:

  • correct alignment of the door - housing
  • correct adherence of the seal to the door

Continue to Step 4

Door fitting faulty

Refit the door correctly ( Op. 7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment)

4

CHECKING MOVING WINDOW GLASS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the moving glass seal In particular, ascertain:

  • the glass is properly closed
  • the glass channel is correctly positioned
  • the glass scraper trim is correctly fitted

Continue to Step 5

Rain gutter fitting faulty

Refit the channel properly ( Op. 7005D18 front side door descending window glass channel r r)

Glass incorrectly fitted

Refit the window mechanism and moving glass correctly (Op. 7005D10)

Glass scraper trim fitting faulty

Refit the glass scraper trim correctly ( Op. 7005D26 front side descending window glass scraper (outer) r r)

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
