With the car in motion, a smell of exhaust gas is noted coming from the rear area.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK TAIL-GATE SEALING STRIP

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tail-gate sealing strip In particular, check the sealing strip for wrinkles

Continue to Step 2

Faulty fitting of seal

Fit the sealing strip correctly ( Op. 7015A30 tailgate compartment seal - r+r)

2

CHECK TAIL-GATE ALIGNMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the alignment of the tailgate, checking that the seal is in contact around the entire perimeter

Continue to Step 3

Tail-gate incorrectly fitted

Refit the tail-gate correctly Op. 7015A14 tailgate - adjust centring and profile - includes hinge and compartment seal adaptation)

3

CHECK BOOT FRAME SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that boot is sealed correctly.

Continue to Step 4

Boot seal faulty

Restore correct boot seal

4

UNDERBODY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the underbody plugs in the boot area are present

Continue to Step 5

Underbody plugs missing

Refit the missing plugs

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
