Removing

Refitting

1. Position adhesive tape (1a) along the entire perimeter of the moulding (1b).

2. Move the moulding (1b) releasing the retainers (2a).

3. Disconnect the electrical connection for the parking sensors.

4. Recover the trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the moulding is undamaged.

Connect the electrical connections for the parking sensors.

Place the moulding in position attaching the retainers.

Remove the protective adhesive tape.
