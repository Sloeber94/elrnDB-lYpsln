Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Position a hydraulic jack under the gearbox.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the power unit lower reaction rod to the front suspension crossmember.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the power unit reaction rod (2b).

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal .

1. Tighten the nuts fastening the rigid section of the exhaust pipe between the catalytic converter and the silencer, silencer side.

Position a hydraulic jack under the gearbox.

2. Undo the bolt fixing the power unit lower reaction rod to the front suspension crossmember.

Remove the hydraulic jack from under the gearbox.

3. Undo the bolt fixing the lower reaction rod for the gearbox side power unit assembly.

To extract the above mentioned bolt, manually rotate the power unit pushing it slightly forwards without affecting the flexible mountings.

4. Remove the lower reaction rod for the power unit.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods
