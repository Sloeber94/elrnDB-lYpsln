The airbag fault warning light is controlled directly by a dedicated connection to the Instrument Panel Node from the Air Bag control unit

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment and check that there are no system/component faults in the Air Bag control unit

Continue to Step 2

System/component fault

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity and the insulation of the fault warning light control lead from airbag control unit to instrument panel See E7030 AIRBAG

Continue to Step 3

Wires in o.c. or s.c.

Renew the wiring See E7030 AIRBAG

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Change airbag control unit Op. 5580C14 air bag ecu - r+r
