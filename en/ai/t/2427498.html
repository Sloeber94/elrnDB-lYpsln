The Customer reports noise from the electric steering system with the vehicle MOVING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON TYPE OF NOISE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the type of noise is not metallic/mechanical over unmade roads

End of diagnosis

The type of noise is not metallic/mechanical over unmade roads

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECK ON CONDITIONS IN WHICH THE FAULT OCCURS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the noise persists even without the driver's Air Bag

Continue to Step 3

The fault does not occur after the driver's Air Bag has been removed

Do not adjust the EPS system, but the Air Bag system

3

CHECK ON CONDITIONS IN WHICH THE FAULT OCCURS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the noise continues unchanged after the steering wheel has been turned in both directions

Continue to Step 4

The noise lessens when the steering wheel is turned in both directions

Replace the EPS system intermediate shaft

4

CHECK ON CONDITIONS IN WHICH THE FAULT OCCURS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the noise alters according to the steering conditions (clockwise rotation, anti-clockwise, steering wheel aligned)

Continue to Step 5

The noise is the same in all steering conditions (clockwise rotation, anti-clockwise, steering wheel aligned)

Replace the insulator (flexible coupling)

5

CHECK ON CONDITIONS IN WHICH THE FAULT OCCURS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the intensity of the noise for clockwise steering

End of diagnosis

The noise lessens when the steering wheel is turned in a clockwise direction

Replace the EPS system
