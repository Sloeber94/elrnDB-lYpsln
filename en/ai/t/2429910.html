Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: Heater

Functional description

The relay T6 in the engine compartment unit B1 manages the switching on of the fan: the coil is supplied by the ignition-controlled line (15/54), while the power line is protected by the fuse F6 of the engine compartment junction unit B1. It is energized by an earth control signal coming from the engine management control unit M10, pin 7 of connector A.

If the engine coolant reaches a first temperature level, the control unit M10 sends a signal from pin 7 of connector A which energizes the fan relay T6 which sends the supply to the fan N11.
