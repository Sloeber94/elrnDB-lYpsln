SPECIFCATIONS

Specifications:

  • splitter ratio equal to 51 mm/fixed revolution
  • steering wheel/wheel angle ratio equal to 14°/1°
  • steering wheel revolutions: ~ 2.6 for a rack travel of 135 mm
  • turning circle: ~ 9.9 m
