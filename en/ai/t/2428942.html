Refitting

Removing

Place the rear heat shield for the exhaust pipe back in its housing and secure it using the retaining clips.

Fix the exhaust silencer to the flexible block.

Tighten the bolt and fit the silencer mount plastic studs.

Refit the central heat shield in its seating, then secure it with the clip and nuts.

Secure the handbrake cables using the retaining bands.

Place the front het shield back in its housing and fix it using the bolts.

Connect the exhaust pipe to the exhaust manifold with catalytic converter.

Tighten the bolt fixing the exhaust silencer front mounting bracket.

Tighten the bolt securing the central exhaust pipe mounting brackets.

1. Tighten the nuts fixing the exhaust manifold and catalytic converter to the exhaust silencer to the recommended torque.
