Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The dipped headlamps do not work in dipped beam position but work in Auto mode.

The dipped beams are controlled by the body computer node via a relay on the control panel.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket and check the following at the Body Computer Node:

  • plausibility of dipped headlamp on/off parameter

Continue to Step 4
