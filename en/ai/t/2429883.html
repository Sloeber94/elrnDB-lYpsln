Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The boot open switch I11 sends an earth signal, when the boot is open, to pin 36 of connector E of M1 to switch on the courtesy light G40.

On the basis of the control logic described previously, the Body Computer M1 supplies the boot courtesy light G40 from pin 23 of connector E.

The 'low battery voltage' signal which switches off the courtesy lights if they are on, reaches the Body Computer M1 via the CAN E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES - Functional description line.
