Reassembling

Dismantling

Check that the satellite and planet gears are intact and that there are no signs of seizing.

Fit the planet gears.

Fit the satellite gears and washers.

Fit the satellite carrier bearing using a suitable drift.

Fit a new satellite carrier bearing retaining pin using a suitable drift.

Using a screwdriver, check that the matching between the planet and satellite gears is correct making sure that they rotate without clearance and with slight resistance.

Fit the ring gear checking the marks made during the dismantling and fix it tightening the bolts to torque.
