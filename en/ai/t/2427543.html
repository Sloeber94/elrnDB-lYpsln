Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the fuel reserve warning light does not come on when the tank is at the reserve level

the fuel level message is supplied by the 'body computer node' and is made available to the 'instrument panel node' via the B-CAN.

the 'body computer node' also sends the fuel level sensor error message.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
