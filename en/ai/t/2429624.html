5580C44 front side bag module one lh or rh r r

1. Disconnect the electrical earth connection.

2. Release the Air Bag module cable retainer.

3. Release the Air Bag cable from the seat structure.

4. Undo the backrest fixing bolts on both sides of the seat.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the backrest frame (1a) after having released it from the guide release control cable (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the backrest frame is not damaged.

Position the backrest and connect the end of the cable.

Tighten the bolts fixing the backrest.

Position the wiring on the frame and secure it using the retaining bands.

In order to prevent any possibility of the wiring being trapped, position it very carefully along the same route as the wiring which has been removed.

Connect the electrical earth connection to the frame.
