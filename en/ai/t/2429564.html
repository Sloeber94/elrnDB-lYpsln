The bead of sealant for the rearscreen can be cut using either harmonic wire or the vibrating knife; both methods are described below.

To cut the bead using harmonic wire, proceed as described below.

1. Cut a section of harmonic wire about 50 cm long, then, using pliers, insert one end (1a) of the harmonic wire through the bead of sealant (1b) which secures the windscreen (1c).

2. Secure the end (1a) of the wire to the retaining tool (2a) located inside the car. Secure the end (2b) of the traction handle (2c) located outside the vehicle.

3. The operator inside the car must keep the end of the tool (2a) on the bead of sealant (3) at about 30 cm from the point of passage of the wire. The other operator outside the car must pull the handle (2c) following the outline of the windscreen, and cut the bead of sealant (3). Position a blade (2d) by the tool (2a) to protect the dashboard trim. Repeat the operation in sections of about 30 cm, until the whole of the bead of sealant (3) is cut.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

To cut the bead using the vibrating knife, proceed as described below.

1. Use the vibrating knife (1a) with blade (1b) and cut the bead of sealant at the bottom of the windscreen working from outside the car.

2. Fit the blade (2a) and cut the sealant on the other sides of the windscreen, working from outside the car.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. After cutting all the bead of sealant, fit the suction handles on the windscreen, then with the assistance of a second operator, remove the windscreen and rest it on a supporting stand.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Using a suitable tool, cut and smooth off the bead of sealant in the windscreen frame, so as to leave a thickness of between 0.25 and 1 mm, without scratching the paintwork. The sealant left in the frame will act as a backing for the new bond.

2. Clean the windscreen seating carefully with compressed air and degrease thoroughly using heptane. If glueing takes place 60 minutes after cutting and smoothing the bead of sealant, treat the sealant surface with the specific activator and wait 15 minutes before fixing the windscreen.

Preparing a new window glass.

1. Degrease the screen-printed edge of the new windscreen using heptane.

2. Using the swab (2a) supplied in the kit, apply the glass adhesion promoter (primer) (2b) on the printed perimeter.

Wait for 15 minutes before carrying out the next operation to give the primer thinner time to evaporate.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Preparing a detached window glass.

1. Using a suitable tool, cut and smooth the bead of sealant on the windscreen so as reduce its thickness to a minimum. The remaining sealant does not have to be removed completely; take care not to damage the black screen printing around the edge of the window.

2. Carefullly clean the printed area around the edge of the window with compressed air and thoroughly degrease with heptane. Check that the window is undamaged. If glueing takes place 60 minutes after cutting and smoothing the bead of sealant, treat the sealant surface with the specific activator and wait 15 minutes before fixing the windscreen.

1. Fit the seal around the perimeter of the windscreen. Use a new seal.

2. Remove the retaining blocks (2a) and make all the pegs (2b) come out completely, then refit the blocks.

3. Remove the adhesive tape from the edges of the bodywork, along the sides and top of the windscreen frame.

4. Test fit the windscreen and centre it perfectly, using the retaining blocks (2a).

5. After centring the windscreen, mark the relative positions of the windscreen and seating, using strips of adhesive tape.

6. Cut the adhesive tape strips and remove the windscreen.

7. Use an air gun to extrude a bead of sealant as evenly as possible along the perimeter of the windscreen.
