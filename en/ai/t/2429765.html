Refitting

Removing

Thoroughly clean sealant residues from mating surfaces between lower and upper crankcase.

Apply the recommended sealant to the entire perimeter of the crankcase sump in an unbroken strip.

Apply a 2 mm thick, unbroken sealant strip to the lower crankcase surface.

1. Position the main bearing halves to the lower crankcase.

2. Fit the lower crankcase to the upper crankcase. check that codes (2a) stamped on both parts of the crankcase correspond because the crankcase components are not interchangeable.

When positioning the lower crankcase, avoid making significant horizontal shifts in position that could remove the sealant.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 16v; all production periods
