Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRELIMINARY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

check

  • that the instrument panel light dimmer has not been selected.
  • that the mode button has not been pressed.
  • that there are no dashes displayed in place of the temperature
  • that there are no failure messages displayed
