Description

The standard type current socket which can be used for connecting instruments or equipment that operate on 12 V is located in the tunnel console.

The ring nut on the current socket is lit with the side lights on E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING .

The cigar lighter resistance can also be located in the socket, to operate it simply press the knob: after a few seconds it pops out automatically, ready for use.

The socket has a heat protection device: if devices with high energy absorption are connected, this protection cuts off the supply.

The current socket circuit is protected by a special fuse and is operated by the relay which is excluded when the ignition is switched on and is located in the junction unit.

The current socket works with the ignition switched ON (INT/A) to prevent the battery from being run down.
