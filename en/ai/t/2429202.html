Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

FOR LEFT RIGID PIPE

Proceed with removal 4450B06 left rear wheel r r .

FOR RIGHT RIGID PIPE

Proceed with removal 4450B08 right rear wheel r r .

1. Remove the protective cap from the bleed screw for the rear brake cylinder.

2. Connect the collection system (pipe and reservoir) to the bleed valve on the rear brake cylinder.

3. Loosen the bleed valve on the rear brake cylinder.

Wait for the brake-clutch reservoir to drain.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
