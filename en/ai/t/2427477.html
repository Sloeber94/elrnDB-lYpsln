PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • the condition of the air filter
  • the state of the spark plugs and leads;
  • battery earth correctly fixed ( See E1010 SUPPLY )

Continue to Step 1

Filter element dirty, damaged or incorrectly fitted

Replace or refit filter element correctly

Spark plugs dirty or damaged

Replace the heater plugs ( Op. 5510D10 spark plugs - r+r)

Spark plug leads damaged

Replace the spark plug leads (Op. 5510D14)

CHECK ON FUEL PRESSURE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, if necessary on the road (to reproduce the conditions in which the fault arises), the fuel supply pressure ( Op. 0510C10 fuel system - check delivery pressure)

Continue to Step 4

Pressure not correct: obstructions in fuel delivery pipe

Remove the obstructions in the fuel pipe, and if necessary replace it

Pressure not correct: tank insufficiently ventilated

Restsore the tank ventilation

Pressure not correct: fuel pump electrical circuit broken

Repair fuel pump electrical circuit See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Pressure incorrect: fuel pump damaged

Replace fuel pump assembly
