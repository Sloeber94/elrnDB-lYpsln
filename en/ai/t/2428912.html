Remove the fuel tank support stand.

Connect the handbrake control cable to the fitting on the rear brake shoes.

Place the protective covers in their housings.

Refit the central heat shield in its seating, then secure it with the clip and nuts.

Secure the handbrake cables to the retaining band.

Tighten the bolt fixing the exhaust silencer front mounting bracket.

Undo exhaust pipe support front bracket securing bolts.

Proceed with refitting 7055B45 right hand rear supplementary wheel arch - r+r .

Proceed with refitting 4450B08 right rear wheel r r .

Fix the fuel supply pipe to the underbody fixings.

Place the protection back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Fix the fuel supply pipe to the front suspension beam using the fastenings.

Proceed with refitting 4450B04 right front wheel r r .

Connect the rapid attachment for the fuel supply pipe.

Connect the electrical connection to the electric pump and fuel gauge sender unit.

Place the protective cover back in its housing and fix it using the bolts.

Put the floor trim back in its place.

Lower the rear seat cushion.

Connect the rapid attachment for the fuel supply pipe to the fuel manifold pipe and secure with the fastenings.

Proceed with refitting 1048A10 air cleaner filtering element - replace .

Proceed with refitting 3380A04 handbrake - check travel .

Proceed with refitting 7040L22 REAR TRIM on TUNNEL -R.R. .

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.

Remove the car from the ramps.
