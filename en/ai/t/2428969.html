1088B32 engine cooling radiator support beam - r r

Path

Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
engines: 1.2 8v, 1.2 16v, 1.4 16v; all production periods

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 1048A10 air cleaner filtering element - replace .

Fasten the radiator to the bodyshell.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the engine cooling radiator supporting crossmember to the wheel arch.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the engine cooling radiator supporting crossmember to the bumper.

3. Undo the bolts fixing the engine cooling radiator supporting crossmember to the bodyshell.

4. Remove the engine cooling radiator supporting crossmember after having released the rubber radiator fastenings.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 16v; all production periods

Removing

Place the engine cooling radiator supporting crossmember back in its housing taking care to secure the rubber fastenings to the radiator.

Tighten the bolts fixing the engine cooling radiator supporting crossmember to the bodyshell.

Tighten the bolts fixing the engine cooling radiator supporting crossmember to the bumper.

Tighten the bolts fixing the engine cooling radiator supporting crossmember to the wheel arch.

Proceed with refitting 1048A10 air cleaner filtering element - replace .

Remove the car from the ramps.
