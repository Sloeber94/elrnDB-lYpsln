Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The side light/number plate light and dipped beams do not work with the exterior light switch on the stalk unit in AUTO position. N.B.: with the AUTO function the side / no. plate lights and dipped headlamps are automatically switched on by the Body Computer Node which receives the command from the dusk sensor (CPS). The sensitivity of the CPS can be adjusted using the 'Mode' control in the centre control panel. If the dusk sensor is faulty and with the C.L.E. on AUTO, the Body Computer switches on the side/no. plate lights and the dipped headlamps.
