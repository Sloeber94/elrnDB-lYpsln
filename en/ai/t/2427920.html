Description

The vehicle is equipped with a reversing light located at the rear in the right light cluster.

It is operated each time reverse gear is engaged through the special switch on the outside of the gearbox casing.

The reversing light circuit is supplied with the ignition key in the ON position (INT/A) and is protected by a special fuse in the underdashboard control unit.

The signal that the reversing light has been switched on is managed by the Body Computer which forwards this information to the steering column switch unit for the automatic operation of the rearscreen wiper E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE and to the parking sensor control unit E3090 PARKING SENSOR
