Removing

Refitting

Open the bonnet lid.

1. Turn the cover retainer.

2. Remove cover (2a), releasing the retainers (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Turn bulb holder (1a), releasing tabs (1b) from retainers (1c), then remove the bulb holder with bulb.

2. Remove bulb (2a) from bulb holder (2b).

If the lamp is working properly, avoid touching the bulb with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Check the condition and the cleanliness of the lamp.

Avoid touching the bulb with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.
