TYRE WEAR CHECK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tyres for wear. In particular:

  • wear on the outside;
  • wear on the inside;
  • wear on the tyre wall;
  • tread wear

Continue to Step 4

Even wear on both sides: tyre inflation pressure low

Restore the correct tyre inflation pressure If necessary, replace the tyres (Op. 4450B21)

Wear on one side (outside or inside)

Continue to Step 2

Wear on the outside: outside smooth

Continue to Step 3

Wear on the tyre walls

The problem is due to the customer's driving style which is too 'sporty': if necessary, replace the tyres (Op. 4450B21)

Wear in the centre of the tread: inflation pressure too high

Restore the correct tyre inflation pressure If necessary, replace the tyres (Op. 4450B21)

Even wear on the surface of the tread: quality of the mixture

Replace the tyres (Op. 4450B21)

Wear not covered by the previous cases

Replace the tyres (Op. 4450B21)
