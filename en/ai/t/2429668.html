7050B12 rear seat head restraint - r r (all)

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the trims (1b) at the back of the backrest cover.

2. Using the side zip fastenings (2a) completely open the side sections (2b) of the backrest cover.

3. Using pliers, remove the end clip (3a) then open the lower zip fastening (3b) along the entire lower section (3c) of the backrest cover.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Lift up the backrest cover (1a) slightly and release the retaining duct (1b) from the frame profile (1c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Lift up the cover (1a) and release it from the head restraint guide bushes (1b).

2. Keep the cover and upholstery.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the velcro for the cover from the upholstery.

2. Release the cover from the perimeter of the upholstery.

3. Using a screwdriver, release the retainers (3a) from the cover metal wire (3b), then separate the cover (3c) from the upholstery (3d).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Using a screwdriver, release the retainer (1a) and extract all the head restraint guide bushes (1b) releasing the positioning tab (1c) from the housing (1d).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the cover is undamaged.

1. Position the backrest cover (1a) and fit the head restraint guide bushes (1b) in position.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Attach the retainers to the metal wire for the upholstery.

Carefully fit the cover on the upholstery and attach the velcro.

Attach the retaining duct to the profile of the frame.

1. To close the zip fastening (1a), use the zip (1b) after having removed the hooks (1c) using pliers.

2. After having closed the zip fastenings (1a), (1d), (1e), in order to prevent them being opened accidentally, fit the retaining hooks (1c) and (1g) to the ends of the actual zip fastenings.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
