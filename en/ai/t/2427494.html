Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON RIMS AND TYRES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • tyre pressures
  • that the rims are not deformed
  • condition of the tyres

Continue to Step 2

Incorrect tyre pressure

Restore the correct pressure

Abnormal tyre wear

Replace deformed rims (Op. 4450B10)

Abnormal tyre wear

Replace deformed rims (Op. 4450B11)

Abnormal tyre wear

Continue according to the instructions in See table X50 TYRE WEAR

2

CHECK ON VIBRATIONS AT LOW SPEED

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Testing the vehicle at low speed, check:

  • that there are no steering wheel vibrations
  • that there is no surging of the car

Continue to Step 3

Rims or tyres ovalized

Replace the deformed part (Op. 4450B10)

Rims or tyres ovalized

Replace the deformed part (Op. 4450B11)

Rims or tyres ovalized

Replace the ovalized part (Op. 4450B10)

Rims or tyres ovalized

Replace the ovalized part (Op. 4450B11)

3

CHECK ON VIBRATIONS AT MEDIUM SPEED

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Testing the vehicle at medium speed (60-80 km/h), check whether there are vibrations on the steering wheel and passenger floor well

Continue to Step 4

Vibrations on steering wheel and floor well

Check that the drive shaft-tripod are in line If the gearbox is out of line, replace it ( Op. 2110A10 manual gearbox and differential - r + r)

4

CHECK ON VIBRATIONS AT MEDIUM-HIGH SPEED

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Testing the vehicle at medium-high speed, check whether vibrations can be felt through the steering wheel or calves

Continue to Step 5

Vibrations on the steering wheel: wheels unbalanced

If possible, rebalance the wheels (Op. 4450B40)

Vibrations under the calves: rear wheels unbalanced

Replace the hubs ( Op. 4450C10 front pillar (one), l/h or r/h complete with wheel hub - r+r with wheel removed - check wheel geometry separately)

Vibrations under the calves: rear wheels unbalanced

Replace the hubs ( Op. 4450D10 hub of one rear wheel, l/h or r/h - r+r with wheel removed)

Vibrations under the calves: rear wheels unbalanced

Balance the rear wheels (Op. 4450B11)

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Check that the fault is no longer present
