When the appropriate on/off button is pressed, the climate control sytem does not work.

The climate control Node supply is protected by special fuses;

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the condition of the climate control system supply protective fuses See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING
  • that the climate control system gas pressure is correct

Continue to Step 1

Fuses blown

Change blown fuses

Relay damaged

Replace relay

Gas pressure not correct

Eliminate leaks and top up climate control system Op. 5040A10

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment and check for errors:

  • at the climate control node;
  • in the CAN line;

Continue to Step 4

Climate control system node not working.

Continue to Step 2

CAN line error.

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment.

2

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the climate control system node power supply See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

Continue to Step 3

No power supply

Restore broken electrical connection See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

3

CHECK ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the electrical connections for the climate control system node, in particular making sure that each individual pin is correctly connected and inserted in its housing.

Continue to Step 4

Electrical connections not working.

Restore damaged electrical connection.

4

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keep the Examiner connected and check the engine control Node for:

  • no errors in the compressor operating relay.
  • no errors in the climate control system pressure sensor
  • no errors at the fan See Test 5040CD Check on climate control fan' speed control signal

Also check the condition of the compressor operating wiring (no short or open circuits)

Continue to Step 5

Relay not working

Change compressor control relay

Wiring faulty

Renew the wiring

Error at linear sensor

Continue to Step 5

Electric fan defective

Replace the fan

Fan wiring interrupted

Renew the wiring

5

COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • that the compressor is correctly activated by See Test 5040BC Check activation/deactivation of climate control compressor from linear sensor

Continue to Step 6

Compressor will not come on

Check and, if necessary, renew the continuity/insulation for the electrical connection between the engine management control unit and the climate control system pressure sensor See E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Replace the climate control system pressure sensor and check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
