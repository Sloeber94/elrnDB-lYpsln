Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle may be equipped with a tow hook with type approval for trailers, caravans, or towing in general.

This device should conform with the laws in force and should be fitted following the instructions enclosed in the fitting kit.

This fitting kit, in addition to the hook itself, also includes an electronic control unit for the operation of the trailer exterior lights as well as specific wiring for connecting the actual control unit with the rear lights of the vehicle and with a specific connection ( D73 ) located in the luggage compartment on the right hand side.

If a trailer is connected, the operation of the parking sensors is automatically inhibited.

The trailer control unit is supplied via the protected circuit by a special fuse in the junction unit under the dashboard.

On some versions/markets the connection ( D73) is not supplied and therefore the fitting kit should have specific wiring with two terminals that are directly connected to the power supply and to the control unit for the parking sensors
