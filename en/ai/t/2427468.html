B13 ENGINE STARTS WITH DIFFICULTY'

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.2 16v, 1.4 16v; all production periods

The engine, especially when cold, starts with difficulty only after repeated attempts. When the key is turned, the instruments light up as normal, the starter motor turns, but the engine has difficulty in starting.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.2 16v, 1.4 16v; all production periods

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • that there are no obstructions in the air filter;
  • the condition of the air intake circuit
  • seal of the diesel pipes;
  • that there is no petrol, water, paraffin or dirt in the diesel filter.
  • the correct operation of the exhaust pipes.
  • the condition of the wiring and operation of the pre-heating system (heater plugs)

Continue to Step 1

Air intake circuit component damaged

Repair or replace the defective component

Air filter blocked

Replace the air filter Op. 1048A10 air cleaner filtering element - replace

Leaks from the diesel pipes

Retighten the pipes, and if necessary replace them.

Presence of water, petrol, paraffin or dirt detected

Clean the tank and pipes and replace the diesel filter cartridge Op. 1044B20 fuel filter filtering element - replace

Obstruction in the exhaust system

Replace the damaged system component

Pre-heating wiring faulty

Renew the wiring See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Heater plugs faulty

Replace damaged heater plugs
