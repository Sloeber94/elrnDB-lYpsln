Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Check:

  • that there are no faults in the Body Computer
  • the operation of the lights in active diagnosis.

Continue to Step 3

No. plate lights error (open circuit or short circuit)

Check the operation of the individual lights: wiring diagram See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

2

CHECK ON INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and complete bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 3

Bulb damaged

Replace the bulbs

Bulb damaged

Repair or replace the bulb holder

3

SIDE/NO. PLATE LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • connections between Body Computer and steering column switch unit;
  • connections between ignition switch, dashboard control unit and Body Computer;
  • connections between Body Computer and bulbs
  • bulb earth: wiring diagram See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 4

Connections broken

Renew the electrical connections See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

4

CHECK ON STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column switch unit is working properly: in particular, the side lights switch:

Continue to Step 5

Stalk unit not working properly

Replace the stalk unit

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
