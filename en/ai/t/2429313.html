Removing

Refitting

Place the vehicle on a lift.

Drain the coolant ( 0010T84 coolant for air conditioning system dehydrate and recharge ).

7210E10 R.R. FRONT BUMPER

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and disconnect the connector (1b) for the pipe from the condenser to the intermediate connector.

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD; all production periods

1. Working in the right front wheel arch, undo the nut (1a) and disconnect the connector (1b).

2. Remove the pipe

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe between the condenser and the intermediate connector is not damaged and there are no cracks which would affect the seal.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors. Only use green coloured seals, resistant to R134A coolant fluid. Lubricate the thread of the connectors using anti-freeze.

1. Place the pipe from the condenser to the intermediate connector in position.

2. Connect the connector (2a) for the pipe and secure it tightening the bolt (2b) to the recommended torque.
