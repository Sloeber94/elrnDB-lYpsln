Refitting

1. Remove the front hub (1a) from the steering knuckle (1b) using a hydraulic press (1c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Position the wheel hub in a vice fitted with protective jaws.

1. Move the bearing inner race away from the front hug using a chisel to allow the extractor arms to be fitted.

When carrying out this operation, take the utmost care not to damage the front wheel hub.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
