Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The earths on the vehicle are divided according to the various wiring they belong to.

Radiator wiring:

  • battery earth, C01 A

Front wiring:

  • left front earth, C10
  • ABS front earth (at left corner), C12
  • electric steering front earth, C14
  • earth on tunnel, C38

Dashboard wiring:

  • passenger dashboard earth, C20 A, C20 B
  • Air Bag system earth, C50

Rear wiring:

  • passenger dashboard earth, C20 C, C20 D
  • left rear earth, C30
  • right rear earth, C31
  • earth for amplifier, C056

Battery negative wiring:

  • battery earth on engine (on gearbox), C02
  • battery earth on bodyshell, C03

Engine wiring:

  • battery earth, C01 A (JTD engine only)
  • earth on engine, C40 (petrol engine only)
  • injection control unit earth, C60 (JTD engine only)
  • compressor earth, C39

Air conditioning wiring:

  • air conditioning unit earth (on right side of dashboard), C16
