Removing

Refitting

Measure the length of the steering rods to be used for refitting.

1. Loosen the nuts fixing the steering rods to the steering box.

2. Undo the steering rods from the steering box and remove them.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the steering rods in their housings, tighten them to the steering box ensuring the lengths measured at the removal are restored.

2. Tighten the nuts fixing the steering rods to the steering box to the recommended torque.
