Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD; all production periods

The crankshaft front fluid-tightness is ensured by an oil seal fitted on the timing cover.

The crankshaft rear fluid-tightness is ensured by an oil seal fitted directly in the seating formed between the crankcase and sub-crankcase.
