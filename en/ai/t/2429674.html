Refitting

7055A40 radiator shield - r r

1. Release the retaining springs.

2. Using a plastic blade, remove the badge (2a) unsticking the double sided tape (2b) along the perimeter.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the crest is undamaged.

1. Clean the badge housing (1a), removing any residual adhesive and degrease with heptane.

2. Remove the protective film (2a) for the double sided tape and place the badge (2b) in position and apply pressure.

3. Attach the clips.
