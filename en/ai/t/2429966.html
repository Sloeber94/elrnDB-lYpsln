WARNINGS

Use care when working on trim components and use the specified tools correctly in order to avoid damage.

Take care to avoid any damage to body paintwork.

Working on the seat belt pretensioners, refer to the warnings for the Air Bags [PR5580C]

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRECAUTIONS

Before working on the electrical system (connectors, electrical components, wiring etc.), turn the ignition off and disconnect the battery.

Working on the seat belt pretensioners, refer to the precautions for the Air Bags [PR5580C]
