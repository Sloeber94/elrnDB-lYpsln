The side brake lights are activated by the Body Computer Node controlled by the brake switch The third brake light is activated directly by the switch

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

BRAKE LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • fuse;
  • connections between DASHBOARD control unit and brake pedal switch;
  • connections between DASHBOARD control unit and bulbs;
  • bulb earth See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 2

Defective component

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections: See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Faulty earth

Restore the earth contact See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

2

CHECK ON BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the brake pedal switch is fitted correctly and working See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 3

Switch fastening / adjustment incorrect

Adjust the switch

Switch fastening / adjustment incorrect

Replace the switch

3

CHECKING FACIA CONTROL UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity of the facia control unit internal circuit See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 4

Facia control unit circuit broken

Change the facia junction unit

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that all the brake lights come on

End of diagnosis
