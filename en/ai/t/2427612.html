With the vehicle moving, there is a noise coming from the lower part of the passenger compartment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the noise is actually coming from the underbody by carrying out the preliminary check for locating noises.

Continue to Step 1

The wind noise comes from the front dooor

Continue according to the instructions in See table T01 WIND NOISE FROM THE FRONT DOORS

The wind noise is coming from the area of the bonnet

Continue according to the instructions in See table T05 BONNET WIND NOISE

The noise is coming from the windscreen area

Continue according to the instructions in See table T06 WINDSCREEN WIND NOISE

The wind noise comes from the sun roof

Continue according to the instructions in See table T20 WIND NOISE FROM THE SUN ROOF

The noise is coming from the rear area

Continue according to the instructions in See table T25 WIND NOISE FROM THE REAR AREA

1

UNDER ENGINE PARTITION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the bulkhead under the engine and make sure that it is correctly fitted

Continue to Step 2

Bulkhead damaged or not fixed correctly

Renew or replace the bulkhead ( Op. 1072C44 exhaust manifold heat shield on bulkhead - r+r)

2

UNDERFLOOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the underfloor cover. In particular, ascertain:

  • The plugs are fitted;
  • The plugs are correctly fitted;
  • Seals present and correct

Continue to Step 3

Faults in the underfloor cover

Renew the plugs and seals for the underfloor cover

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
