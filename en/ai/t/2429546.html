Removing

Refitting

Open the bonnet lid.

1. Unscrew the nuts.

2. Disconnect the rod from the two retainers.

3. Keep the lock.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the catch is undamaged.

Connect the rod to the two retainers.

Position the lock and screw in the nuts without tightening them.

1. Adjust the catch position.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Tighten the nuts

Check that the bonnet lid is opening and closing properly.
