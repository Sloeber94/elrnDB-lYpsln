The Customer reports occasional hardening during the rotation of the steering wheel (no power assistance), without the failure warning light coming on

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.2 16v, 1.4 16v; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

INTAKE AND EXHAUST SYSTEM LEAKAGE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the intake and exhaust system visually and by listening, to check that there are no leaks downstream of the MAP sensor/flow meter.

Also take into account:

  • Blow-by circuit;
  • Canister circuit, checking that the valve is not stuck open;
  • Brake servo vacuum pipes;
  • Injector O-Rings

Continue to Step 2

There are leaks

Eliminate leaks from the system. Replace faulty parts if necessary

Continue to Step 5

2

PETROL PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Turn the key to MAR and check that fuel pump activates.

Whilst the pump is activated, check that the fuel pressure is not too low

Continue to Step 3

Low pressure: fuel filter blocked/obstructed

Replace the fuel pump Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 5

Low pressure: supply pipe restrictions/leaks

Renew or, if necessary, replace the pipes involved

Low pressure: pressure regulator defective

Replace the fuel pupm Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 5

Low pressure: pressure regulator incorrectly calibrated

Replace the fuel pump Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 5

Low pressure: electrical problem in the fuel pump

Low pressure: electrical problem in the fuel pump See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

If necessary, replace the fuel pump Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 5

Low pressure: excess pressure valve stuck open

Replace the fuel pupm Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 5

3

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Continue to Step 4

Impurities present

Clean the tank Op. 1040A24and introduce new fuel.

Continue to Step 5
